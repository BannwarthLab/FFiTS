Fit diagnostics — product structure, full Newton (off-diagonal Hessian)

Convergence

Objective value per Newton iteration. Should fall roughly monotonically and flatten out; a plateau followed by a jump, or persistent oscillation, signals a step that's too large or a Hessian direction that isn't behaving.

Parameter movement

Final force constant relative to its starting value (log scale), one dot per parameter, grouped by term type. A dot at 0 didn't move; far left means it collapsed toward zero. Points in red moved to below 1% of their starting value.
